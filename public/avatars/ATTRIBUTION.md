# Avatar asset attribution

The eight built-in avatars are generated from original Three.js geometry and project-authored color and proportion presets. They do not include third-party meshes, textures, photographs, or generated video.

No external VRM file is distributed in this release. The manifest and `VrmAvatar` adapter only load a VRM when a future role entry includes all of the following: source URL, author, exact license, redistribution permission, and modification permission.
